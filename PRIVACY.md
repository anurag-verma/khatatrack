# KhataTrack Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

## Summary

KhataTrack is an offline expense tracker. Your financial data stays on your device. We do not operate servers, accounts, or analytics that collect your information.

## Data We Collect

**None.** KhataTrack does not send data to KhataTrack or any third party.

## Data Stored on Your Device

- Transactions (amount, category, payment mode, notes, tags, date)
- Budgets, recurring reminders, and split expense records
- App preferences (language, theme, display name)
- Optional SMS hashes to prevent duplicate imports

All data is stored in a local SQLite database (`khatatrack.db`).

## SMS Permission (Android)

If you enable SMS scanning:

- The app reads bank/UPI SMS from your inbox to suggest transactions
- Parsing happens entirely on your phone
- You review and approve each import before it is saved
- SMS content is not uploaded anywhere

You can deny SMS permission and use manual entry or paste-SMS instead.

## Backup

When you export a backup, the JSON/CSV file is created on your device. You choose where to share it (WhatsApp, email, etc.). KhataTrack does not receive these files.

## App Lock

If enabled, biometric or device PIN is handled by your phone's operating system. KhataTrack does not store your PIN.

## Children's Privacy

KhataTrack is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of changes.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the project repository.
